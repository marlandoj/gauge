# Gauge v2 diagnostic preregistration

Tune only on the original 60 development tasks and operator-approved D01–D25. Preserve the original held-out dataset and 40% result. Freeze the implementation, scoring code, rubric and development inputs before a separate agent authors fresh diagnostic tasks. That author must not inspect the classifier, development cases, predictions, or earlier heldout. Use 30 distinct scenarios, six per tier, labeled by the unchanged original rubric. Record every task and rationale, author, actual creation time and file digest. No production text or provider calls on the classifier path.

The separate author is an agent, not a human reviewer or an independent model/vendor panel. This cohort measures generalization to independently authored synthetic wording only. It cannot meet the requirement for a human-reviewed holdout until a human explicitly reviews the labels. Permission to continue does not supply those labels. Do not claim production usefulness, significance or promotion from this small balanced English-only diagnostic.

Score all cases once against the frozen production classifier, the archived v1 classifier, and both current local/swarm baselines. Check source and dataset digests, actual assessment parity, and baseline stability. Record a no-overwrite opening receipt before loading fresh tasks. An interrupted opening requires inspection; never delete the receipt to retry. After opening, freeze remains fixed; failures inform a future candidate using a different holdout. Exact duplicates and development group overlap are rejected; semantic overlap needs a reviewer and cannot be proven by string checks.

Use the original metrics and acceptance function without changing thresholds: exact accuracy at least each baseline, fewer complex/apex under-routes, no increase in errors of two or more tiers, no complex/apex abstention. Score preserved fallback results as well. Also report v1 on the same fresh tasks, per-tier confusion and recall, group accuracy, abstentions and four-tier boundary normalization. A comparison between 40% on the old holdout and a new percentage on this cohort is not a controlled before/after comparison.

All seven harnesses continue through the shared assessor and resolver. Verify production caller preservation, pin precedence, privacy, off/unsupported modes and cross-process receipts. Publish test scope and skips. Measure new-process CLI and observer timings separately from warm inner-function timings. Keep routing shadow-only. Human label review and separate live-routing authorization remain prerequisites for promotion.
