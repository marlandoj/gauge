# Gauge v3 preregistration

This is a new candidate under the original Gauge evaluation contract. Tune on the original 60 development tasks, the 25 approved D cases and the 30 approved H cases only. H01–H30 were opened and reviewed after v2 results; for v3 they are development material, never independent validation. Preserve all v1/v2 artifacts and an exact archived v2 classifier. Do not read or train on the original heldout.

Compare a broader local lexical approach on grouped development folds, and use development evidence to select the candidate. No provider requests, downloads, runtime cache or external model dependency. No claim that development performance measures generalization. One production classifier serves CLI, observer and evaluation; reason strings remain controlled codes. Existing shadow timeout and privacy contracts remain unchanged.

Freeze source, scoring, rubric, development inputs and this protocol before fresh authoring. A separate agent with no parent history receives only the original rubric and this protocol. It authors 40 distinct synthetic scenarios, eight per tier, with IDs N01–N40, unique group IDs, task text, tier and rationale. Vary wording, domain and placement of requested outcome; use realistic distinctions in reasoning difficulty rather than inserting a tier name into the task. Record actual creation time, author identity, digest, counts and lack of human review in a separate manifest. No production prompts. The author must not inspect source, development examples or previous predictions. This is separate-author synthetic evidence, not an independent vendor panel or human judgment.

Score once after reserving a no-overwrite opening receipt, verifying the freeze, source/development digests, manifest, 40 distinct groups, eight cases per tier and no exact development duplicates. String/group checks do not prove semantic independence. Compare frozen production v3 with archived v2 and both canonical local/swarm baselines on every input. Verify assess parity and baseline stability. Keep the original metrics and acceptance function unchanged: accuracy at least each baseline, fewer complex/apex under-routes, no increase in severe errors, no complex/apex abstention. Report raw and operational fallback comparisons, confusion, tier recall and group scores. Zero baseline misses makes strict improvement impossible; never redefine that gate after opening.

After opening, do not retune v3, discard cases, change labels or rerun to select better results. Preserve failures for the next candidate. Measure fresh-process CLI and observer latency separately with 20 samples, count all missing receipts. Complete mechanical, seven-harness and five-part gap checks. Publish evidence and a human label-review table; new labels remain proposed until the user explicitly reviews them. Even a diagnostic pass is not authorization for live routing. No promotion or changes to ZOU-1685.
